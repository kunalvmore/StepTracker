Privacy Policy

Step Tracker is built so your data never leaves your iPhone. Here’s exactly what that means.

The short version

Step Tracker doesn’t collect, store or share any of your personal data. There’s no account and no server. Everything the app shows you is worked out on your iPhone, and stays there.

No accountNo sign-up, email or password.
No data collectionNo analytics, no tracking, no servers.
No adsNothing is sold or shared.
Stays on your iPhoneEverything the app stores lives on your device.

Apple Health

With your permission, Step Tracker reads these from Apple Health:

  • Steps
  • Walking and running distance
  • Active energy
  • Exercise minutes
  • Flights climbed

It uses them only to show your progress, streaks, awards and widgets. With your permission, it also saves walks you track in the app to Health as Walking workouts.

Health data is never used for advertising, never sent off your device, and never shared with anyone. You can change these permissions any time in the Health app: tap your profile picture, then Apps → Step Tracker.

Motion & Fitness

During a walk you start in the app, Step Tracker uses your iPhone’s motion sensors to count steps and pace live. This data is processed on your device and isn’t stored anywhere else.

What’s stored on your iPhone

  • Your settings, such as daily goal, theme and reminder time
  • Your walk history and earned awards
  • A small summary of today’s numbers, so the widgets and Live Activity can show them
  • The photo you choose for the Photo widget, if you pick one

All of this stays inside the app on your iPhone. It isn’t uploaded or backed up to any server we run.

Notifications

If you turn on reminders, they’re scheduled on your iPhone. No notification data leaves your device.

What we collect

Nothing. Step Tracker has no analytics, no tracking and no third-party code that collects data.

If you’ve chosen to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone’s settings, Apple may send us anonymous crash reports. They don’t identify you, and you can turn this off in Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements.

Third parties

We don’t sell, rent or share any data, because we don’t have any. Downloads are handled by Apple through the App Store.

Children

Step Tracker doesn’t collect personal data from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Delete the app and everything it stored on your iPhone is removed. Walks saved to Apple Health stay in Health, where you can manage or delete them.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we’ll update this page and the date at the top. Questions? Email mkunal065@gmail.com.
