Common questions

Getting started

My steps show 0. What do I do?

Step Tracker needs permission to read Apple Health. Open the Health app, tap your profile picture, then Apps → Step Tracker, and turn on Steps and the other categories.

Why is my count different from the Health app?

Both read the same data, but Health can merge iPhone and Apple Watch steps a moment later. Pull down on Today to refresh.

Do I need an Apple Watch?

No. Your iPhone counts steps on its own. Apple Watch steps come in through Health too.

Walks & Live Activity

The Live Activity doesn’t show on my Lock Screen.

Go to Settings → Step Tracker and turn on Live Activities. Then start a walk from the Walk tab.

Steps stop updating while my phone is locked.

iOS updates Live Activities in short bursts to save battery. The timer stays live, and steps catch up within a few minutes or when you unlock.

Widgets

How do I add a widget?

Touch and hold your Home Screen, tap Edit → Add Widget, and search for Step Tracker. On the Lock Screen, touch and hold, tap Customize, then the widget area.

My widget isn’t updating.

Open Step Tracker once so it can save today’s numbers. After that, iOS refreshes widgets through the day.

Reminders

I’m not getting reminders.

Check Settings → Notifications → Step Tracker. Reminders also skip days you’ve already hit your goal.
